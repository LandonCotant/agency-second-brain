## Purpose of This Document

This is the structured output of the Rule the Room goal-setting exercise. It captures the goal hierarchy from mission down to quarterly building blocks. It serves as:

1. The reference document both partners work from
2. The seed of the Goals table in Airtable when the Brain build starts (week 1-2)
3. The context the Triage Agent reads when classifying inbound items as actionable or non-actionable
4. The artifact to revisit at quarterly retrospectives and the start of each new quarter

Goals are nested: each layer derives from the layer above. When in doubt about whether a task or commitment "fits," check it against the relevant layer. If it doesn't trace upward to mission, it probably shouldn't get attention.

## Mission

> **Agency helps high-stakes, underserved practitioners win more of the work they're already qualified to do — by applying data science, AI, and craft-level marketing to industries where those tools have not yet arrived.**

**Status:** Working draft (v0.1). Both partners agree it captures the structure, with minor wording tweaks possible later.

**Notes on what this means:**
- "Helps" — service language, not product language. Reflects "people at the center."
- "High-stakes, underserved practitioners" — captures both sophistication and niche profile.
- "Win more of the work they're already qualified to do" — Agency is a force multiplier on existing capability, not a substitute for it.
- "Craft-level marketing" — Peninsula's site copy is the bar; not template work.
- "Industries where those tools have not yet arrived" — captures the timing arbitrage.

---

## Business Structure (Operating Reality)

The mission is executed through three revenue streams sitting on one underlying capability:

**Underlying capability:** Applied data science + ML + craft-level marketing/web execution, run by the operator and partner with whatever team and contractors are needed.

**Revenue stream 1: Vantage / Agency Services**
Retainer work for e-commerce, local, and agency-partner clients. Predictive ML platform plus hands-on execution. The proven engine. Pays the bills, generates case studies, sources potential partnership prospects.

**Revenue stream 2: Niche Service Partnerships**
Peninsula Judgment Recovery is venture #1. the operator and partner are the marketing/operations co-founders; the licensed practitioner (Client A for Peninsula) is the service expert. Revenue share rather than fee-for-service. **Strategic priority for growth.** Future ventures follow the same model.

**Revenue stream 3: Project Work**
Websites and one-off engagements. Opportunistic. Cash-flow positive. Useful but not strategically central.

When tradeoffs arise, partnership-building energy is preserved at the expense of project work — never the other way around.

## 3-Year Vision (Committed v0.1)

**Both partners have committed to this as the 3-year vision.**

In three years, Agency operates as a small, sustainable platform business with two layers.

**The bottom layer** is the Vantage agency book — e-commerce, local, and agency-partner clients on retainer — run with intentional restraint, primarily as a relationship origination and case-study engine for the partnership work, not as a growth target in its own right. the operator and partner can run it themselves or contract it out as needed.

**The top layer** is a portfolio of 2-4 niche service partnerships in the Peninsula model — the operator and partner as the marketing and operations co-founders, the licensed practitioner as the service expert, revenue share rather than fee-for-service. Peninsula is fully proven out and producing predictable revenue share by year one; partnership two and three are added in years two and three based on a defined ideal-partner profile that emerges from doing the work, not from theorizing about it.

**the operator completes the Ross MBAn program in year one** and uses it as an active R&D function for Agency — every meaningful project, paper, and case study is selected to also produce something useful for the business.

**Operating capability is built deliberately across all three years:** systems get codified, the implicit service standards get written down, and the team grows from "the operator + partner" to "the operator + partner + maybe a couple employees" as the partnership portfolio justifies it. ML and data science capability deepens through doing the work rather than through frontier research.

**The business stays sustainable:** the operator and partner work fewer hours overall by year three, with most of that time spent on strategy, data science, and partnership oversight rather than direct operations.

The mission throughout is consistent: helping high-stakes, underserved practitioners win more of the work they're already qualified to do, by applying data science, AI, and craft-level marketing to industries where those tools have not yet arrived.

## 1-Year Commitment (Committed v0.1)

**Both partners have committed to this as the 1-year commitment.**

By month 12, Agency accomplishes four things.

**First**, Peninsula Judgment Recovery is proven out — both as a revenue floor (a defined monthly recurring revenue level to be set in week-1 planning, after Peninsula has 2-3 more months of data) and as a replicable playbook documented in enough detail that a future niche-service partner could be onboarded against it.

**Second**, the Brain ships and is operational, with both the operator and partner using it daily by week 5 of the build, and with the system running stably through the start of MBAn so it actively reduces operational load during the program rather than competing for attention with it.

**Third**, MBAn coursework is biased toward business application — the operator uses meaningful projects, papers, and case studies as direct R&D input to Agency where natural, accepting that some coursework will be standalone.

**Fourth**, the Vantage / agency book holds steady or selectively grows — clients are only added if they fit the partnership-source profile, meaning they're plausible candidates to become Peninsula-style partners in 18-24 months.

**What is explicitly NOT in year one:**
- Partnership #2 (reactive only — if the right partner finds us, we'd evaluate; we don't actively hunt)
- Service standards codification (deferred to year two when there's more data to codify from)
- "Complete MBAn" as a tracked goal (it's a given, not a goal)

## Quarterly Building Blocks (Q2 2026 — Pre-MBAn Quarter)

**This is the working surface.** The Triage Agent will read these goals when classifying every inbound item as actionable vs. non-actionable. They are intentionally small in number and intentionally concrete.

The next 12 weeks are the operator and partner's only "full operational capacity" window before MBAn intensity changes the rhythm. These three goals are designed to use that window well.

### Building Block 1: Ship the Brain v1

**Owner:** Both
**Parent (1-year):** Brain shipped and operational pre-MBAn
**Definition:** The 12-week build per the Brain Build Spec is executed on schedule. By end of quarter, all six agents are operational, both partners are using the system daily, and the operational drag reduction is measurable.
**Metrics:**
- 12 build weeks completed
- Both partners receiving and using morning briefs and evening reflections by week 6
- At least one Risk Watcher flag confirmed as a real catch
- Brain audit log shows no HIPAA isolation breaches
**Anti-goals (what not to do):** No scope creep beyond the spec. No deferring v1 capability to v2 just because the build is going well. No skipping the goal-intake transcription.

### Building Block 2: Peninsula — Sign 5 New Attorney Firms

**Owner:** Both
**Parent (1-year):** Peninsula proven out
**Definition:** Five new California litigation attorney firms signed as Peninsula clients this quarter.
**Metrics:**
- 5 new firms signed (binary count)
- Pipeline data captured well enough to set the year-1 revenue floor at the next planning session (~Aug 2026)
**Anti-goals:** No vanity engagement metrics that don't translate to signed firms. No expanding scope into other Peninsula adjacent verticals — focus on the existing positioning.

### Building Block 3: Goal Hierarchy + Airtable Foundation in Place

**Owner:** Both
**Parent (1-year):** Brain shipped and operational pre-MBAn
**Definition:** This goal hierarchy is loaded into Airtable as actual records. The full Airtable schema per the Brain Build Spec §3 is implemented and validated. Sync from Airtable to BigQuery is operational and verified to run reliably for 48+ hours before the Triage Agent goes live in week 3.
**Metrics:**
- All goal records in this document exist in Airtable Goals table
- All schema tables per spec §3 created and validated
- 48-hour clean sync run completed before week 3
**Anti-goals:** No "we'll fix the schema later" shortcuts. No adding ungoverned fields that the agent has to guess at.

## Cross-Cutting Operational Notes

These aren't goals but they're constants that shape how the goals get pursued.

**On work division:** the operator and partner have "fuzzy lanes" today. Both agree this is acceptable for now but worth refining over time. No formal lane definition this quarter; informal coordination continues. The Brain will help surface drift.

**On the partner relationship:** This is a 50-50 partnership. All goals require both partners' agreement. The Brain is rolled out to both partners simultaneously in week 5, not sequentially. Both partners get the same data with personalized views; neither has privileged access to operational visibility.

**On the operator's time allocation:** the operator's stated 3-year aspiration is "fewer hours overall, lots of strategy and data science." Today, the operator is "mostly running operations" with a small portion of time on his actual unique value (data science, ML, marketing strategy). This gap is the core operational pain the Brain is built to close. Reducing the operator's operational hours is a measurable success criterion for the Brain itself.

**On the Peninsula revenue floor:** Deferred to a planning session in approximately 90 days, after Peninsula has 2-3 more months of operating data. This deferral has a forcing function: the Goal Steward will surface this decision as a flag at day 90.

**On HIPAA clients:** Strictly excluded from the Brain. Any client flagged HIPAA in Airtable does not appear in any Brain table, agent output, or notification. Multiple enforcement layers per spec §11.

## Cadences

These are the cadences the Goal Steward agent will run once operational.

**Weekly (every Friday):**
- Each Quarterly building block scored 1-10 by the owning partner(s)
- One-sentence "why this score" recorded
- Goal Steward flags any building block dropping 2+ points week-over-week
- Goal Steward flags any goal not scored in 14+ days

**Quarterly (end of each 12-week period):**
- Review completion rate of quarterly building blocks
- Set next quarter's building blocks
- Suggest carry-overs for goals that didn't complete but are still relevant
- Update the 1-year commitment if needed
- Produce a quarterly retrospective document

**Annually:**
- Full review of 3-year vision against reality
- Update mission wording if needed
- Set the next year's commitment
