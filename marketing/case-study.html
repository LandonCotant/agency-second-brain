The Challenge

A two-person marketing agency was losing leverage to operational overhead. Client-risk signals — a slipping response, an owner disengaging, a deliverable met with silence — were only noticed after they became churn. Inbound work piled up unclassified. And the "who said what, when" context that keeps accounts healthy lived in one founder's head, email, and a dozen Airtable views. Enterprise ops tooling was overkill; doing nothing was costing accounts.

The Solution

We built a multi-agent operations brain on Google Cloud that runs continuously and proactively. Independent agents each own one job — classify, watch for risk, extract commitments, capture facts, brief the founder — writing structured outputs to a shared warehouse. A conversational layer lets the founder ask the whole corpus in plain English.

  • Triage agent — classifies every inbound signal and drafts the next action in minutes.
  • Risk watcher — scores each client against segment-specific churn patterns, daily.
  • Memory layers — automatically extracts commitments, facts, and relationships from the day's activity.
  • Knowledge surfacer — hybrid search over the full history, answered with citations.
  • Daily brief & reflection — a composed, ranked view of what needs attention.

Human-in-the-loop by design

Every agent is drafts-only — it prepares email replies, tasks, and CRM updates for one-click human approval, and is architecturally blocked from sending or writing to systems of record on its own. Sensitive / regulated accounts are isolated at the infrastructure boundary.

The Results

  • Churn risk surfaced early. Disengagement and "silent-after-deliverable" patterns are flagged automatically before they become cancellations.
  • Operational synthesis off the founder's plate. A composed daily brief replaced manual inbox-and-Airtable triage each morning.
  • Institutional memory that answers back. Client history, open commitments, and entity facts are now queryable in natural language — retrieval accuracy tuned to near-perfect on real queries.
  • Runs itself for the price of a phone plan. The entire system operates unattended at ~$50/month, guardrailed by automated cost and security audits.
  • Built pragmatically. Shipped in weeks by a single implementer — no enterprise ceremony, no vendor lock-in.

How It's Built

Portable, model-agnostic core
Agents do deterministic classification; the frontier LLM composes. Swap models with one config change.
Open-protocol interface (MCP)
17 tools exposed over an open standard — usable from phone, web, or desktop.
Governed & auditable
Every classification, flag, and draft is logged; least-privilege access enforced on every change.
Standard cloud data warehouse
No proprietary black box — data stays queryable and yours.

What this looks like beyond a two-person agency

The same architecture scales from a lean shop to a multi-team client-services operation. The pattern — proactive monitoring, AI triage, and an answerable institutional memory — maps onto any business where account health lives in email, CRM, and calendars.

Account retention
Flag at-risk clients — slipping engagement, silent deliverables, cooling buyers — before renewal season.
Ops triage
Auto-classify inbound requests and draft the next step for human approval — not autonomous send.
Shared memory
One queryable brain over email, CRM, and calendars so knowledge survives handoffs and turnover.
